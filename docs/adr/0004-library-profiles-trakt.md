# ADR 0004: Library, household profiles, Trakt and TMDB

- Status: accepted, built for Phase 3 on the branch `claude/project-thread-11r23t`
- Date: 28 October 2026

## Context

Phase 3 turns BannerTV from something one person browses into something a household lives in. The plan's Phase 3 box lists a library, profiles with a PIN, Trakt sign-in and scrobbling, TMDB enrichment, Home row reorder and settings backup; Continue Watching and auto-pick moved into Phase 2 and are done. On 28 October 2026 Eyitayo asked to start Phase 3 before PR #4 and PR #6 merge, so it is built on the playback branch the way Phase 2 was built on Phase 1, and the Home screen stays as Phase 1 tuned it on the TV: no new requests, artwork sizes or link tests on Home.

Three facts shaped the design:

- The app keeps its data in module-level stores (progress, played cards, preferences, the addon collection, query caches, focus memory) that screens import directly. Swapping all of them in place for another person would leave something of the first person behind somewhere.
- Trakt answers browser requests only from origins registered with the app, and a packaged webOS app runs from `file://`, whose origin is `null`. Its device sign-in also needed the app's client secret, which anyone can read out of an app package. (Since October 2026 Trakt issues no secret to new apps and the device sign-in needs only the client ID, so the Worker sends a secret only when one is set; it still keeps the client ID out of the package and the repository.)
- Addon catalogs say almost nothing about who a title is for. Cinemeta gives IMDb genres, including Family, and can filter its catalogs by genre; age ratings come only from TMDB.

## Decision

1. **Profiles are local, up to six.** Each has a name, a colour, an optional four-digit PIN and a kids flag, kept in localStorage (`bannertv.profiles`) so the app can decide what to show before the first paint. The first profile, "Main", is the person who has used BannerTV so far: it keeps the IndexedDB keys and preferences it already has, so nothing is migrated and an older build still reads them. Every other profile's keys carry a `p/<id>/` prefix (`p/k3x9/progress`), and its preferences live in `bannertv.preferences.<id>`. Deleting a profile deletes its keys.
2. **What is whose.** Per profile: watch progress, Continue Watching cards, the library, recent searches, Trakt, track choices, Home row layout and preferences (stream choice, link testing, subtitle languages and style). Shared by the TV: the addon collection, the response cache, which addons have played on this TV, and the connection's bitrate limit. Addons stay shared because a household has one debrid account and nobody should paste its key six times; kids profiles see a filtered view of them (item 5).
3. **Choosing a profile.** With one profile nothing changes: there is no picker and no avatar. With two or more, "Who's watching?" shows at launch (Settings can turn that off, and the last profile used opens instead), and an avatar at the foot of the rail opens it again. Per-profile stores wait for the choice: the profile-scoped key-value store holds every read and write until a profile is chosen, so nothing about the wrong person is read or written. Switching profile mid-session stores the choice in sessionStorage and reloads the app, which starts every module-level store, cache and focus memory afresh; a reload keeps the sessionStorage choice, so the picker does not ask again. The reload also names the profile in the address (`#profile=<id>`), because a storage write made just before a reload can be lost.
4. **PINs.** Four digits, typed with the remote's number keys or an on-screen pad. Stored as a salted SHA-256 hash (a small JavaScript implementation, since `crypto.subtle` is not promised at `file://` on webOS 5), never the digits. Five wrong tries lock the pad for 30 seconds, counted across launches, and a clock moved back does not stretch the wait. A PIN keeps a person out of a profile and out of its settings; it is a household lock, not security against someone with the TV's developer tools. A forgotten PIN is cleared from another profile without one, or by clearing the app's data.
5. **Kids profiles.** A kids profile has a highest age rating (U, PG or 12; PG by default) and:
   - sees only films and series, from addons not marked adult (`behaviorHints.adult`);
   - gets Home rows built from catalogs that can filter by the Family genre (Cinemeta's Popular and Featured films and series), and no other catalog rows;
   - sees, in every row, grid and search result, only titles whose genres include Family or Kids and none of Horror, Thriller, Crime or War (titles with no genres are left out), so a filter that fails leaves a title out rather than letting it in;
   - on Details, when TMDB knows the title's UK certificate (or a US rating mapped to one), Watch is offered only at or below the profile's rating, with a plain note otherwise;
   - has no Addons section, no Trakt, and Settings shows only subtitle style; the rail's avatar switches profile. Leaving a kids profile asks for the PIN of the profile being opened, so parents give their own profiles a PIN.
6. **Library.** "My List" per profile: a toggle on Details (`+ My List` / `In My List`), and the Library section, which shows My List or History, of films, series or both. My List is sorted by date added, name or newest release; History lists every title watched, most recent first, from the progress and Continue Watching cards already kept. A saved title keeps what a tile needs (name, poster, backdrop, logo, year, rating, genres), so the Library opens without asking any addon. At most 1,000 titles.
7. **Audio and subtitles remembered per title.** Choosing an audio or subtitle language in the player saves it for that title (a series counts as one title) and, as before, as the profile's default. The next stream of the same title starts with that title's choice, other titles with the default. At most 300 titles, the least recently used dropped first.
8. **Home row layout.** Settings has Home rows: every catalog and genre row, with Show or Hide and Move up or down. The layout is kept per profile by a key that survives reordering addons (addon id, type, catalog id and genre), and applied to `buildBoard`'s rows in one function before Home draws them. Rows the layout does not know (from an addon added later) keep their place after the known ones. Continue Watching stays first. Home itself is not otherwise changed.
9. **A small BannerTV service for Trakt and TMDB.** A Cloudflare Worker (`services/api`, deployed as `api` beside the pairing relay) holds the Trakt client ID (and secret, only for a Trakt app that has one) and the TMDB key as Worker secrets, so neither the app package nor the repository holds them. The TV calls it with simple requests (GET, or POST with a plain-text JSON body) so there is never a preflight, and it answers with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`. It passes on only the Trakt and TMDB paths BannerTV uses, adds the keys, caches TMDB answers for a day at the edge, and keeps no logs, since requests carry the viewer's Trakt token. `/status` says which services have keys; the app hides Trakt and TMDB until they do.
10. **Trakt sign-in.** Settings, Trakt shows the code and a QR code for trakt.tv/activate, and the TV asks every few seconds (at Trakt's interval) until the viewer approves, the code expires or the viewer leaves. Tokens are kept per profile in IndexedDB, never logged; access tokens last a day, so the TV refreshes them a little before they run out and on a 401, and a refused refresh signs the profile out with a note. Sign-out also revokes the token.
11. **Scrobbling.** The player tells Trakt when a film or episode with an IMDb or TMDB id starts or resumes, pauses, and stops (leaving, the end, or the next episode), with the percentage watched; Trakt marks it watched at 80% or more. Nothing is sent for the Lab, a live broadcast, or a title Trakt cannot identify (Kitsu ids, for example). A request that fails is not retried later; the next start or stop carries the position.
12. **Trakt sync.** At launch (once Home has settled), every 30 minutes while the app is on screen, and after the player closes, the TV asks `sync/last_activities` and fetches only what changed:
    - paused films and episodes (`sync/playback`) become progress entries when newer than what the TV has, so Continue Watching and Resume pick them up, and their cards come from the meta addon as for older progress;
    - watched films and episodes (`sync/watched`) give the watched ticks on Details and episodes; the latest episode watched in the last 30 days of each show becomes a finished progress entry, so its next episode can join Continue Watching;
    - the watchlist and My List are kept the same both ways: titles added or removed on the TV are sent at once (and again at the next sync if that failed), and what changed on Trakt since the last sync is applied on the TV.
13. **TMDB enrichment on Details only.** When a title has an IMDb id, Details asks TMDB (through the service) for the matching film or series with its images, cast, age ratings and, for a series, the season shown. It adds what the addon left out and never replaces what it gave: a title logo and a backdrop without text, cast with photos, the UK certificate, the network or studio, and episode stills and summaries. Answers are kept for a week in the response cache. Settings can turn it off. Home, Discover and Search never ask TMDB, so browsing costs nothing more. Settings carries TMDB's attribution under the version line.

## Consequences

- With one profile, BannerTV looks and behaves exactly as before. Profiles cost a reload to switch, about the time of a launch.
- Main keeps its keys, so a downgrade to Phase 2 still finds its data; other profiles' data is invisible to it.
- Kids profiles lean on IMDb's Family genre where TMDB has no rating. That leaves out some titles children could watch (an Animation title not tagged Family), which is the safer mistake. Addons other than Cinemeta add no rows to a kids Home.
- Trakt and TMDB depend on BannerTV's service being up. Until `/status` says a service has its keys, its settings stay hidden and BannerTV works as before; once shown, a service that cannot be reached is named in the message on the Trakt screen, and Details shows the addon's own details.
- The service is public, so anyone could call its TMDB path with BannerTV's key. It passes on only BannerTV's paths and methods, which keeps it from being a general proxy; Cloudflare's free plan covers a household many times over.
- Not in Phase 3: settings backup to a phone (the pairing relay only passes data from phone to TV, so it needs a change to the relay), browsing community addon catalogs, per-title reuse of the last link, Simkl and MDBList, and cloud sync between TVs (Phase 5).
