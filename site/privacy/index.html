Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

BannerTV has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking. Almost everything it knows stays on your TV. This page explains the few things that leave it, and where they go.

The short version

  • Your settings, addons, list of recent searches and watch progress are stored on your TV, not sent to me.
  • When you browse, search or play, your TV talks straight to the addons you've added, to the sites they point to and to a few services BannerTV uses for artwork. They see your IP address and what you ask for, including what you type in Search. Apart from BannerTV's own demo addon, which keeps no logs, those requests don't come to me.
  • While you're adding an addon or changing its settings, a small relay I run lets you do it from your phone. It holds an address you send in memory only, forgets it within about 11 minutes and keeps no logs.
  • Trakt and crash reports only work if you turn them on.
  • I don't sell data, show adverts, use analytics or build profiles of anyone.

What leaves your TV, and who receives it

The addons you add

BannerTV has no content of its own, apart from the demo addon described below. It asks the addons you've added for catalogues, title details, streams and subtitles, and searches them as you type once you've typed two letters. It also re-checks each addon's details once a day, shortly after BannerTV starts. These requests go directly from your TV to each addon's server and include your IP address, the titles or episodes you open, what you type in Search, and anything in the addon's address, such as an account key. Other people run these addons and decide what to do with those requests under their own terms and privacy policies.

BannerTV comes with three addons already added: Cinemeta (titles and artwork) and OpenSubtitles v3 (subtitles), which Stremio runs, and BannerTV Demo, which I run. You can remove any of them.

The BannerTV Demo addon

The demo addon offers three short films from the Blender Foundation, which are free to share under Creative Commons licences, so there's always something legal to play. A Cloudflare Worker at demo.bannertv.workers.dev serves its listings, films, artwork and subtitles. When your TV browses, searches or plays it, Cloudflare handles the connection as my processor. That includes your IP address, the film or file your TV asks for and, when you search, the words you type, which are part of the request's address. The addon uses those words only to pick matching films from its three, and keeps nothing: request logs, traces and log exports are all turned off, so I can't see who watches or searches for what.

Artwork, streams and subtitles

Posters and backdrops load from the image services your addons point to. BannerTV also fills in missing backdrops and title logos from Metahub (images.metahub.space), an image service run by Stremio, which sees your IP address and the IMDb number of the titles on screen. Films, episodes and subtitle files load from wherever the addon's link points. Those services see your IP address and the file your TV asks for.

The phone relay

Typing a long address with a remote is slow, so BannerTV lets you send it from your phone through a small relay I run at pair.bannertv.workers.dev:

  • While the Add an addon window, or an addon's Settings window, is open on your TV, your TV gets a six-character code from the relay, shows it with a QR code and checks back every 3 seconds. So the relay sees your TV's IP address even if you type the address on the TV instead.
  • If you scan the code, your phone opens the relay's page and sends the address you paste. The relay holds it in memory only, never on disk, and passes it to your TV.
  • The relay forgets the address 30 seconds after your TV has it, in case the first delivery fails. Each code lasts 10 minutes, and nothing is held more than about 11 minutes after the code was made. Apart from that, the relay stores only a scrambled copy of the TV's session secret and the expiry time.
  • Request logging is switched off. I can't see the addresses that pass through.

The relay runs on Cloudflare's global network, usually in the data centre nearest you. Cloudflare, Inc., a United States company, handles the connections, including your TV's and phone's IP addresses, as my processor under its data processing addendum. An addon's own settings page, which opens from the QR code, is run by that addon, not by me.

Trailers

The Trailer button opens your TV's YouTube app with that trailer. From then on YouTube's own terms and Google's privacy policy apply.

Trakt (optional)

If you link a Trakt account, BannerTV sends Trakt what you start, pause and finish watching, and reads your watch history and lists from it, so Trakt can keep your history in step across your devices. This goes directly between your TV and Trakt under Trakt's privacy policy. BannerTV keeps the Trakt sign-in token, and the history and lists it reads, on your TV until you unlink Trakt or remove the app. When you unlink Trakt in BannerTV's settings, BannerTV deletes all of them from the TV. You can also revoke BannerTV's access in your Trakt account settings, and delete what Trakt holds there. BannerTV uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt.

The Movie Database (TMDB)

BannerTV may fetch extra artwork, cast and episode details from TMDB for the titles you see. Those requests go directly from your TV to TMDB and include your IP address and the title being looked up. BannerTV uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB.

Crash reports (optional, off unless you turn them on)

If you turn on crash reports in Settings, BannerTV sends a report to Sentry when something goes wrong. A report holds the error, the BannerTV version, your TV's model and webOS version, and the name of the screen you were on. It never includes addon addresses, account keys, the web addresses BannerTV was loading or what you were watching, and your IP address is removed before a report is stored. Reports are kept in Sentry's EU region (Frankfurt, Germany) by Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry), a United States company, as my processor, and deleted after 90 days at most. You can turn crash reports off again at any time, and nothing more is sent.
