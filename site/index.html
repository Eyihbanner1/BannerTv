BannerTV

A media centre for LG webOS TVs. It plays films and series from the Stremio-compatible addons you choose, and it's built for the LG remote and Magic Remote.

Apart from BannerTV Demo, a few Creative Commons films from the Blender Foundation for trying the app, BannerTV doesn't host or provide any films or series. Everything else comes from the addons you add, and you're responsible for the addons you choose.

Getting BannerTV

BannerTV is free and is coming to the LG Content Store in the United Kingdom. It needs an LG TV with webOS 5.0 or newer, which covers most LG TVs from 2020 onwards.

Help

How do I add an addon?

Open Addons from the menu on the left and choose Add an addon. Type the addon's address with the remote, or scan the QR code with your phone and paste the address there. BannerTV then shows the address on the TV so you can check it before adding it.

Why can't I find a title?

BannerTV only shows what your addons provide. If a title is missing, it isn't in any of your addons' catalogues.
